Rentgh ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rentgh mobile application. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

By using Rentgh, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you register
  • Listing information: Property details, photos, location, and pricing when you create a listing
  • Messages: Chat messages exchanged between renters and property owners
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app (searches, views, filters used)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your property listings to potential renters
  • To enable communication between renters and owners
  • To improve the app and fix issues
  • To send important service updates (not marketing spam)

3. Firebase Services

Rentgh uses Google Firebase to power core functionality:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages your account login securely
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores listings, messages, and user profiles
  • Firebase Storage: Stores property photos you upload

Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Firebase. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only:

  • With other users as part of the service (e.g. your name and listing details are visible to renters)
  • With Firebase/Google as our infrastructure provider
  • When required by law

5. Property Listings

When you post a listing, the property details (title, description, price, area, photos) are publicly visible to all app users. Your account email is never shown publicly — only your display name.

6. Messaging

Chat messages between renters and owners are stored in Firebase Firestore. Only the two participants in a conversation can read their messages. We do not read private messages except to investigate reports of abuse.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days. Listing data may be retained in anonymised form for analytics.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

Rentgh is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and Firebase's built-in security rules to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
